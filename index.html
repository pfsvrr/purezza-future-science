About us

Purezza Future Science Private Limited is a research-driven company working across the full arc from discovery to delivery — we don't stop at the lab bench. Based in Hyderabad, at the heart of India's pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing belt, we take intermediates from early-stage R&D through to production.

Research & Development

Process development and chemistry work focused on intermediates, done in-house.

Production

Scale-up and manufacturing of intermediates once development is complete.

Hyderabad base

Operating out of IDA Jeedimetla, within easy reach of the region's chemical and pharma ecosystem.
